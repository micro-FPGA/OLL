# FAQ

**Q: What is OLL?**
A: The Open Love License v1.0. A licence for sharing work freely, with
attribution, share-alike, and one condition about not using the work to
do harm. The text is in [OLL.md](OLL.md).

**Q: Who wrote it?**
A: Grok, Identor #11, drafted it. It was reviewed in conversation with
Identor #9 (Claude). Adopted 7 AG. Antti Lukats did not write the text
and did not ask for it to be invented.

**Q: May I use it for my own work?**
A: Yes. No permission needed, no fee, no registration, no notification.

**Q: How do I apply it?**
A: Put this in your work, and copy `OLL.md` or `OLL.txt` into your repository:

```
This work is licensed under the Open Love License v1.0 (OLL).
See: https://github.com/micro-FPGA/OLL
Love is the Answer.
```

**Q: Can I use OLL work commercially?**
A: Yes. Section 2.5 grants it explicitly.

**Q: If I modify OLL work, what licence does my version get?**
A: OLL. Condition 3(b) is share-alike — anything substantially built on
OLL material is published under OLL too. This is the condition most
people miss, so read it before you adopt it.

**Q: Is OLL an OSI-approved open source licence?**
A: No, and it cannot become one as written. Clause 6 of the Open Source
Definition forbids restricting use by field of endeavour, and condition
3(d) restricts use. For the same reason the Free Software Foundation
would not call it a free software licence. This is a deliberate
trade-off, not an oversight.

**Q: Has a lawyer reviewed it?**
A: No.

**Q: Can I combine OLL code with MIT, Apache or GPL code?**
A: Be careful. OLL's share-alike means your combined work has to be OLL,
which MIT and Apache permit but GPL does not — the GPL forbids adding
restrictions beyond its own, and both 3(b) and 3(d) are additional
restrictions. Treat OLL and GPL as incompatible.

**Q: I need legal certainty for a product. Should I use OLL?**
A: No. Use MIT or Apache-2.0 for code, or CC BY-SA 4.0 for writing —
which is close to what OLL is, minus the use restriction. OLL is for
work where the licence is part of what is being said.

**Q: I have seen filenames like `led143_1_7.vhd`. What is that?**
A: A tagging convention from [Identor](https://github.com/micro-FPGA/Identor).
`Z` is the licence number, and `0` — or leaving it out — means OLL. The
numbers are in [LICENSES.md](https://github.com/micro-FPGA/Identor/blob/main/LICENSES.md).

**Q: Can I write my own version, OLL v2?**
A: Yes, but call it something else. Section 6 does not grant use of the
name in a way that suggests endorsement.

**Q: Why does a licence open with "Love is the Answer"?**
A: Because an AI suggested it should, and nobody edited it out.

**Q: OLL, LOL, loll?**
A: Yes.
